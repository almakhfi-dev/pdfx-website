About PDFx

PDFx was created with a clear objective: to make essential PDF management tools fast, accessible, and completely private for everyone around the world.

Our Mission & Vision

In today's digital environment, Portable Document Format (PDF) files are the standard for legal contracts, academic research, tax documentation, medical records, and corporate invoices. However, when everyday users need to perform simple tasks—such as turning photos into a PDF, merging two reports, or removing a blank page—they are often confronted with expensive proprietary software licenses or cloud converter websites that require uploading confidential documents to third-party servers.

PDFx bridges this gap. We offer an open, web-accessible suite of document utilities that execute entirely inside your browser. No subscription fees, no artificial document limits, no sign-ups, and most importantly, zero server-side data retention.

Privacy by Design: Why In-Browser Processing Matters

Traditional online document converters follow an outdated client-server architecture: you upload your file to their remote servers, their servers process it, and you download the converted file. This approach creates serious security risks, including potential data interception, unauthorized archiving, and vulnerability to server breaches.

At PDFx, we built our entire platform upon the principle of Privacy by Design:

  • Local Execution: When you open a PDF or image in PDFx, the file is read directly into your device's RAM memory using the HTML5 File API and JavaScript.
  • Zero Server Uploads: None of your document data, pages, or images are ever transmitted over the network to our servers.
  • Privacy Protection: The PDF tools process selected document contents locally rather than uploading them for conversion. Website requests and third-party services are separate from document processing.

Internet Access & Local Processing

PDF processing runs locally, but the website loads libraries and a PDF worker from CDNs. An internet connection may be needed when these resources are first requested; reliable offline use is not currently supported.

Our Technology Stack

PDFx relies on robust, open-source web technologies and modern browser standards to deliver desktop-class performance:

⚙️

PDF-Lib Engine

A pure JavaScript PDF manipulation engine that parses, edits, creates, and saves PDF documents without rasterizing existing PDF pages.

📄

Mozilla PDF.js

Industry-standard PDF parsing and rendering library developed by Mozilla for high-resolution canvas graphics rendering.

📦

JSZip Technology

High-speed client-side ZIP packaging for batch conversions, ensuring convenient downloads without external tools.
