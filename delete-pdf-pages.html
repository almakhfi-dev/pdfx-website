How to Delete Pages from a PDF

Cleaning up unnecessary sheets from your PDF file is straightforward with PDFx:

1

Select Your PDF

Click the file selection button and pick your PDF. The tool displays thumbnail previews for each page in order.

2

Mark Pages to Delete

Click on each page you wish to remove. Selected pages are clearly highlighted for verification.

3

Download Refined File

Choose the removal button to generate a PDF containing the unselected pages.
