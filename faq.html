Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PDFx tools, document-processing practices, supported formats, and troubleshooting.

General & Pricing

What is PDFx?

PDFx is a free, web-based suite of document utilities designed to handle everyday PDF tasks—such as image-to-PDF conversion, PDF-to-JPG rendering, file merging, page splitting, deletion, reordering, and page extension—directly in your browser.

Is PDFx really 100% free?

Yes. All current features on PDFx are free to use. There are no hidden fees, paid tiers, or requirement to input credit card information.

Do I need an account or email to use PDFx?

No. You can use any tool instantly without registering an account, providing an email, or setting up a password.

Are there watermarks added to my files?

Never. PDFx does not add any watermarks, stamps, or advertising banners to your documents.

Privacy & Security

Are my files uploaded to your servers?

No. PDFx processes files exclusively inside your browser using client-side JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. The tools do not upload document contents for conversion. Hosting, CDN, and advertising services may receive standard web requests.

Is PDFx safe for sensitive legal and financial documents?

Local document processing avoids uploading files for conversion. It is not an absolute security guarantee; use a trusted device and browser and follow your organization's requirements.

Does PDFx comply with GDPR and privacy standards?

Local document processing reduces the need to transfer document contents. It does not by itself establish GDPR or other regulatory compliance. See the Privacy Policy for hosting, CDN, and advertising data practices.

What happens to my files after I close the tab?

Closing or reloading the tab ends the tool session. The browser manages memory release; PDFx does not guarantee immediate secure erasure. Downloaded files remain on your device until you remove them.

Tools & Technical Capabilities

What is the maximum file size I can process?

Because processing runs locally on your machine, there are no artificial file upload limits. You are only limited by your device's available RAM.

Which image formats are supported?

Our image tools support standard JPEG, JPG, and PNG formats. They preserve colors and high resolutions accurately.

Can I rearrange and rotate pages simultaneously?

Yes. In the Rearrange PDF tool, you can drag and drop page thumbnails to reorder them and use the ↶ / ↷ buttons to rotate individual sheets.

Can PDFx handle password-protected PDFs?

Currently, PDFx processes unencrypted PDF documents. If your document requires a password to open, please remove the password before using our tools.

Troubleshooting & Support

Why did my browser slow down on a massive file?

Very large PDF files (e.g., hundreds of high-resolution scanned pages) require considerable memory to render. If a slowdown occurs, try splitting the document into smaller sections first.

Which web browsers are supported?

Use a current browser such as Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. Compatibility, downloads, and drag-and-drop behavior can vary by browser and device.

Can I use PDFx on my smartphone?

The layout adapts to mobile screens. Large files and drag-and-drop controls may work better on desktop. Android users can also explore our PDFx app on Google Play.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please send an email to almakhfi.dev@gmail.com with a brief description of the issue or feature request.
