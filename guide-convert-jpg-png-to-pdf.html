For a three-page handwritten assignment, capture each sheet separately with good lighting and readable margins.

Steps in PDFx

Open JPG / PNG to PDF

  1. Open JPG / PNG to PDF and select the three image files.
  2. Review the thumbnails, drag them into order, and rotate any sideways image.
  3. Create the PDF and zoom into handwriting or small print to check readability.

Before you share the output

A photo exported into a PDF remains an image. Use an OCR workflow if you need text search or selection. Convert HEIC images to JPG or PNG first.
