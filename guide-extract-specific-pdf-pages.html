Extract specific pages from a PDF

If a packet is 14 pages long and a colleague needs pages 4 and 7, read the thumbnail positions carefully. A printed page number can differ from its position in the file.

Steps in PDFx

Open Split PDF

  1. Open Split PDF and select the source PDF.
  2. Select thumbnails 4 and 7. Verify that exactly two pages are selected.
  3. Choose “Create Selected PDF” and confirm that pages 4 and 7 appear in the download in that order.

Before you share the output

Extracted pages may lose context from the original. Keep the source file and review the new PDF before sending it. Use Delete PDF Pages when you want to keep everything except a short list.
