Protect your privacy when using online PDF tools

Decide whether your organization allows the document to be opened in a browser. Use a non-sensitive sample first if you need to understand a tool’s behavior.

Steps in PDFx

Open Merge PDF

  1. Read the PDFx Privacy Policy for how the site handles selected files, third-party requests, and contact messages.
  2. For an approved browser-based task, open the matching tool, select only the files you need, and check the output before sharing it.
  3. Remove temporary copies from shared devices and check where your browser saves downloads.

Before you share the output
