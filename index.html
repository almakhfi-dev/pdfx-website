Why Choose PDFx? Private, Secure & Client-Side

Most online PDF services require you to upload your confidential files to their external servers. This poses substantial privacy and security risks, particularly for legal contracts, medical reports, financial statements, and personal identity documents.

PDFx is built differently: Our tools leverage modern browser APIs (such as WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and client-side JavaScript libraries including PDF-Lib and PDF.js). When you choose a file, it is processed directly inside your browser's local sandbox memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about how PDFx handles your documents? Here are answers to the most common inquiries:

Are my files uploaded or stored on any server?

No. PDFx processes files exclusively inside your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your files never touch our servers, guaranteeing complete confidentiality.

Is PDFx completely free to use?

Yes. All PDF utilities on PDFx are 100% free with no hidden paywalls, subscription requirements, or watermarks added to your documents.

Can I convert scanned documents and photos into PDF?

Yes. You can upload multiple JPG or PNG image files, rotate them to the correct angle, reorder the sequence, and compile them into a standardized PDF.

What is the file size limit on PDFx?

Because processing runs locally on your machine, there are no artificial server upload caps. You are only limited by your device's available memory.

Do I need to install any software or extensions?

No software installation is necessary. PDFx works directly on Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Safari, and mobile web browsers.

Are output PDFs compatible with Adobe Acrobat?

Yes. All generated PDF documents follow standard ISO PDF specifications, making them fully readable in Adobe Acrobat, PDF viewers, printers, and cloud drives.
