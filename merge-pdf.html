Why Merge PDFs Locally in Your Browser?

Managing multiple separate PDF attachments is one of the most frustrating document bottlenecks in modern workflows. Whether submitting multi-part financial audits, compiling student research papers, or organizing contract addenda, sending individual pages clutters inboxes and invites misfiling. A unified PDF presents a polished, professional deliverable that is easy to read, archive, and print.

How Client-Side PDF-Lib Merging Works

PDFx utilizes PDF-Lib, a high-performance pure JavaScript library that manipulates the internal cross-reference tables (XRef tables), object streams, and font dictionaries of PDF documents. When merging documents:

  • The binary bytes of each selected PDF file are parsed directly in your browser's Web Worker or thread.
  • A brand new target PDF document is created in memory.
  • Pages from each source file are copied over losslessly along with their vector instructions, fonts, and embedded images.
  • The unified document is saved and delivered straight to your downloads folder without contacting any cloud API.

Complete Privacy for Confidential Documents

Legal agreements, accounting ledgers, medical records, and corporate disclosures should never be uploaded to unverified third-party cloud servers. Because PDFx executes 100% locally on your device, your private document data never leaves your computer or phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about merging PDF files on PDFx:

How many PDF files can I merge at the same time?

There is no arbitrary restriction. You can select multiple files at once. The tool effortlessly merges multiple documents together.

Does merging reduce document quality or resolution?

No. PDFx copies the vector streams, text glyphs, and images directly without re-compression. The visual fidelity remains identical to the originals.

Are my confidential files uploaded to any server?

No. All merging operations take place entirely inside your web browser. Nothing is ever sent across the network or stored in the cloud.

Can I merge PDF files with different page orientations?

Yes. If one PDF has portrait pages and another has landscape pages, PDFx preserves the individual dimensions and orientation of each page.

Do I have to pay or create an account?

Never. PDFx is 100% free with no registration, no subscription fees, and no watermarks added to your merged documents.

What happens if one of my PDFs is corrupted?

If a file has structural errors, the browser will alert you so you can replace the damaged file before merging the rest.
