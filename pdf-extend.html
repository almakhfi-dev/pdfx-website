How to Extend a PDF with Additional Pages

Appending new sheets to an existing document is fast and effortless with PDFx:

1

Choose Existing PDF

Select your original PDF file. PDFx parses and displays its existing pages in the visual grid.

2

Add & Position Images

Click "+ Add JPG / PNG Pages" to load photos or scans. Drag them to your preferred placement in the document.

3

Generate & Download

Click "Extend PDF". Your browser merges the original pages and new images into a cohesive PDF file.

Formats and troubleshooting

PDF plus JPG or PNG images → one PDF

Insert a photo of a signed form into an existing packet.

Add the original PDF first, then add the JPG or PNG pages. Drag the new thumbnails to the intended positions before choosing Extend PDF. Confirm the page count and check that each image is legible.
