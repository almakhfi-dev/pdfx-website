Why Use PDF Extend to Augment Your Documents?

It is very common to finalize an important PDF—such as a contract, research report, or job application—only to discover that an additional signed verification page, photo identification, or purchase receipt needs to be appended. In the past, users had to convert the entire PDF to Word, insert the picture, re-format the layouts, and export a new PDF, frequently ruining formatting.

Seamless Image Embedding with PDF-Lib

PDFx solves this problem with the PDF Extend tool. Built directly on top of PDF-Lib, PDF Extend copies existing PDF pages and adds images as new A4 pages. Added images are resized to a maximum edge of 1700 pixels and JPEG-compressed, with transparent areas placed on white. It does not overlay images on existing PDF pages.

Complete Privacy for Important Documentation

Appending ID cards, utility bills, or signed signature pages involves sensitive personally identifiable information (PII). Uploading these records to unverified remote web servers poses serious identity theft and security concerns.

With PDFx, all image embedding and PDF extension take place strictly in your browser's private local environment. The tool does not upload document contents for processing. Standard website, library, and advertising requests may still occur.

Flexible Page Ordering

You are not restricted to appending new pages to the end of your document. Thanks to our drag-and-drop preview system, you can insert new sheets at the beginning, between pages 3 and 4, or anywhere else you need.

Common Scenarios for Extending PDFs

  • Appending ID Proofs: Insert photos of driver's licenses, passports, or social security cards into submitted application packets.
  • Attaching Expense Proofs: Add scans of receipts or invoices to existing corporate expense reports.
  • Adding Signature Pages: Append handwritten or photographed signature sheets to digital contract drafts.
  • Late Addenda & Notes: Add updated charts, project addenda, or errata sheets to finalized presentations.
