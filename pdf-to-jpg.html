High-Fidelity PDF Image Extraction with Client-Side Safety

PDF documents are designed for static cross-platform consistency, but sharing individual charts, slides, or diagrams often requires a versatile image format like JPG. Whether you want to post a PDF flyer on social media, embed an invoice illustration in an email, or insert a slide into a presentation, converting PDF pages to JPG is the ideal solution.

How PDF.js and Canvas Ensure Pristine Image Quality

PDFx utilizes Mozilla's industry-standard PDF.js rendering engine combined with the HTML5 Canvas API. When you load a document, our software parses the vector paths, typography fonts, embedded images, and color matrices inside the PDF. Each page is drawn onto a dedicated high-DPI virtual canvas, ensuring that fine text lines, mathematical formulas, and photography remain sharp without blurriness.

Why Local Browser Processing Protects Your Sensitive Information

Bank statements, payslips, medical records, tax filings, and legal agreements are frequently formatted as PDFs. Uploading these documents to random third-party web services exposes your confidential data to data breaches, scraping, and unauthorized server storage.

With PDFx, your document never leaves your machine. The rendering, image encoding, and ZIP file compilation take place entirely within your browser's isolated sandbox memory. No copies or logs are ever made on remote servers.

Smart Packaging: Single Image or ZIP Archive

When you convert a single page, PDFx conveniently downloads a single ready-to-use .jpg file. When converting multiple pages, PDFx automatically bundles them into an organized .zip file using client-side JSZip for convenient extraction.
