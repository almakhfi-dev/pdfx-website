PDFx Privacy Policy

How the website handles selected files, network requests and support email.

Files and downloads

The tools read selected images and PDFs in your browser and pass generated files to your browser for download. The tool code does not upload selected files to a processing server. Your browser and device control where downloads are stored and how long they remain there.

Website requests

Vercel hosts the website. unpkg and cdnjs provide libraries and a PDF worker. They can receive connection information such as IP address and browser headers when your browser asks for resources. The source contains no dedicated analytics tag.

Advertising and cookies

The site includes Google AdSense. Google and its partners may place or read cookies, use web beacons or IP addresses, and collect information to deliver, personalize or measure advertising, depending on applicable settings. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Email and privacy requests

If you send email, your address and message are shared with PDFx and handled by email providers. Please leave out sensitive files. For questions about retention or requests concerning your privacy rights, email almakhfi.dev@gmail.com.
