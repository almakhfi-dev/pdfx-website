Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Your privacy is paramount. Discover how PDFx processes your documents with zero remote storage.

1. Introduction

PDFx ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website located at https://pdfx-website.vercel.app/. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our online PDF tools and related web services.

We are firmly committed to user privacy. Our fundamental architecture relies on client-side execution, meaning your documents and files are never transmitted to or stored on our web servers.

2. Document Files & Processing (Zero Server Retention)

When you use PDFx to convert images to PDF, convert PDF pages to JPG, merge documents, split pages, delete pages, rearrange layouts, or extend PDFs:

  • Local Processing: All file conversions and transformations occur strictly inside your web browser's local sandbox memory using client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and the HTML5 Canvas API.
  • No Server Uploads: Your documents are never uploaded to our servers, cloud databases, or third-party storage providers.
  • No Permanent Storage: Once you close or reload the browser tab, all loaded document data in your browser's RAM memory is immediately released and discarded.

3. Log Data and Analytics

Like most website operators, our hosting provider (such as Vercel) may collect basic non-personally-identifying technical information transmitted by your browser. This may include your browser type, language preference, referring site, operating system, and the date and time of each visitor request. This data is used solely for traffic monitoring, security diagnostics, and system stability.

4. Cookies & Google AdSense Advertising

We may use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide personalized advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Libraries

PDFx loads established open-source client-side libraries from trusted Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) including Cloudflare cdnjs and unpkg (such as PDF-Lib, PDF.js, and JSZip). These CDN providers may log standard request IP addresses for security and caching purposes according to their respective privacy terms.

6. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), PDFx acts in accordance with the GDPR:

  • We do not collect personal data from your uploaded files.
  • We do not create user profiles or track individuals across sessions.
  • Because no document data is transferred to our servers, no cross-border data transfer of your files takes place.

7. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not retain or sell personal data derived from your uploaded documents.
  • You have the right to request information regarding any analytics data logged by visiting our contact page.

8. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, such as the Google Play Store for our Android application. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational practices or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
