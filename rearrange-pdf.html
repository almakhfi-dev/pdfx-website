How to Rearrange and Rotate PDF Pages

Fixing out-of-order scans or inverted pages is simple and fast with PDFx:

1

Upload Your Document

Select your PDF file. The browser renders interactive page thumbnails in their current sequence.

2

Drag & Rotate

Click and drag any page thumbnail to move it earlier or later in the document. Click rotation arrows to adjust orientation.

3

Download Reordered PDF

Click "Rearrange PDF". The browser generates your reorganized PDF document ready for immediate download.

Formats and troubleshooting

PDF → one reordered PDF

Put a batch of scanned forms into chronological order.

Drag page thumbnails into the desired sequence and use rotation controls to fix sideways pages. Verify both orientation and order in the exported copy. If dragging is difficult on a touch screen, try a mouse or trackpad.
