How to Split a PDF Step by Step

Extracting only the necessary pages from a bulky document takes less than 30 seconds with PDFx:

1

Load Your PDF

Select your PDF file using the file selector. PDFx generates interactive visual thumbnails for every page.

2

Click Target Pages

Click on the page thumbnails you want to extract. Highlighted pages will be included in the new document.

3

Create & Download

Click "Create Selected PDF". Your browser compiles a lightweight, clean PDF containing only your chosen pages.
