Can I choose non-consecutive pages?

Yes! You can select any combination of pages—for example, pages 1, 4, 7, and 12. PDFx will assemble them cleanly into your new PDF.

Not at all. Your original file on your computer remains untouched. PDFx creates a brand new document containing only your extracted pages.
